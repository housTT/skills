Size coordinator turns by their resolved provider and reasoning effort, with a modest prompt-size allowance and a one-hour hard ceiling. A project's own `turn_timeouts_s` entry wins, then a flat `turn_timeout_s` (a stored 600, the old default, counts as unset), then the built-in table. Record timeout diagnostics and split large blocker sweeps into durable, bounded batches with complete item coverage across consecutive turns; a sweep queued before the upgrade keeps items that cleared meanwhile, so their outcome is still recorded.
