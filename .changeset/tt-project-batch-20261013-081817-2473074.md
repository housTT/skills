`tt-project`: ttp prune archives what finished worktrees hold (hand-off artifacts, untracked files under a cap) and removes them; clears stale initializing locks; frees reviewed worktrees once the review ends; the disk guard sweeps before it holds work; the worktree sweep saves a done task's tracked changes as a verified patch under the disk guard (or ttp prune --patch-tracked), archives small ignored logs, and indexes archives in MANIFEST.md; archive writes go through durable_write/write_json/durable_append; the tarball is synced before its rename; archive.store is transactional (space check against the disk guard, pending manifest, rollback, recover); the daemon archives one worktree per tick step under a time budget with progress; tests for the transactional archive and the bounded daemon sweep; operate.md describes both and the pending-manifest gap; a failed archive does not use up the daemon's one-per-step slot, and a forced (disk-guard) re-sweep does not retry it every tick; cross-filesystem big-file copies report progress per 64 MB; exempt archive.copy's synced .part write in the durable-write audit.
